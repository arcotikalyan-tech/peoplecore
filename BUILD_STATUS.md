## Vertical slice: what exists

**Database** (`supabase/migrations`)
- Tenancy: organizations, members, platform admins.
- RBAC: per-org roles seeded from templates, permission catalog, org/team/self scopes, time-boxed grants.
- Append-only audit log: trigger-written, diffed updates, redaction support, blocks UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE.
- Org structure: legal entities, locations, cost centres, departments, designations.
- Employees, plus effective-dated employment history. Overlaps are blocked by an exclusion constraint.
- Sensitive employee data: `employee_personal` and `emergency_contacts` (RLS; employees edit their own); identity numbers and bank accounts in the `restricted` schema, which the API can't reach, read and written only through permission-checked functions (`employee_identity_summary` / `reveal_employee_identity` / `set_employee_identity`, and the bank equivalents). Summaries are masked; every reveal is audited. Only Aadhaar's last 4 digits are stored. Changing a bank account archives the previous one.
- Employee documents: `employee_documents` (RLS read; writes only via `add_employee_document` / `delete_employee_document`; `document_download` checks access and records the download). Files sit in the private `employee-documents` bucket (5 MB; PDF, JPEG, PNG) with no Storage policies for signed-in users, so only the server reaches them, via 60-second signed URLs. Deleting hides the record (kept for the audit trail) and removes the file.
- Employee export: CSV of the directory with its current filters (work fields only; never personal, identity or bank data), up to 50,000 rows, formula-injection-safe. `log_employee_export` records each export (filters, row count) before any data is sent, and refuses without `employee.export`.
- Employee import: CSV upload (template provided; headers in any order; DD/MM/YYYY accepted), up to 2,000 rows and 1 MB. "Check file" is a true dry run: `import_employees` creates every row as the caller inside a subtransaction, reports problems by line, and rolls everything back. The import itself is all-or-nothing and audited. Managers are matched by employee code, from the file (any order) or the directory.
- Recruitment (slice 2, migration 20261018001400): offers with immutable versions (CTC redacted in the audit trail), approved through the approvals engine by someone else with `recruitment.offer.approve`, then sent and answered; hiring now requires an accepted offer and takes its joining date and designation (emits `employee.hired`). Interviews with panelists (who get a task and a notification, see only name/role/round, and see others' scorecards only after submitting their own), scorecards rated per criterion. A public careers page: the only anonymous access (two functions, no tables; published open requisitions; consent; email-only matching; throttled; same answer whatever the case), threat model T17.
- Recruitment (slice 1): `job_requisitions` (auto `REQ` codes, hiring manager, openings, status), `candidates` (deduplicated per org by email and phone; consent recorded; personal values redacted in the audit trail), `applications` (stage pipeline; stage changes only through `move_application`; an application can't be re-pointed). `hire_application` creates exactly one employee per application (pre-joining for a future date), placed from the requisition, and closes the requisition when all openings are filled. `erase_candidate` deletes a never-hired candidate. Managers see requisitions for themselves and their reporting line.
- Platform services: job queue (`app.jobs`: idempotency keys, leases, `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, exponential backoff, dead after max attempts; worker API granted to the service role only), domain events (`app.domain_events`, each enqueuing an `event:<type>` job in the same transaction), in-app notifications (own only; only `read_at` is writable), tasks (assignee, their line and HR see them), and a generic approvals engine: flows per subject type with ordered steps (reporting manager with a permission fallback, or a permission), maker ≠ checker, the subject can't approve, nobody approves two steps, rejections need a reason, requesters can withdraw, module handlers run on the outcome, approvers and requesters are notified.
- Leave: holiday calendars (holidays, weekly offs; per location or the org default), leave types with versioned effective-dated policies (monthly/yearly accrual, carry-forward cap, eligibility, probation), an append-only ledger (balance = sum, never stored), requests that count working days on the employee's calendar, reserve balance while pending, can't overlap, and are approved by the reporting manager (else `leave.approve`) through the approvals engine; cancelling before the start reverses the debit; HR adjustments; accrual (pro-rated for mid-year joiners on yearly policies) and year-end lapse in the worker. Defaults per organization: CL, SL, EL (no accrual during probation), LOP.
- Attendance: shifts (org default) and effective-dated roster assignments; raw, immutable punches with a source key (client punch id or device serial + record), so retries and offline sync record once (future punches refused, offline punches accepted for 72 hours and flagged when late); geofences per location (punches outside or without a location are flagged, never refused); derived attendance days (present, half day, absent, leave, weekly off, holiday) recomputed from punches, shifts, calendars and approved leave, including night shifts; regularization through the approvals engine (approval adds punches and recomputes the day); biometric CSV import read in the org's time zone; anomaly flags with review; a reproducible monthly summary for payroll (payable and LOP days) that recomputes its month first.
- Onboarding: templates (tasks for the joiner, their manager or an owner, due relative to the joining date), plans whose tasks go to the shared task system and that complete themselves, started automatically for every hire by the worker (`employee.hired`) or by HR; versioned policies with append-only acknowledgements and an HR status view. Defaults: a "Standard onboarding" template per organization.
- Payroll (slice 1): salary components (Basic, HRA, Special by default), effective-dated compensation with lines (amounts redacted in the audit trail), statutory profiles (PF, ESI, PT state), global statutory rule sets as versioned data (bundled FY 2025-26 and provisional FY 2026-27 sets, both marked unvalidated). Runs per month: `payroll_run_inputs` assembles compensation, attendance-based loss of pay (unpaid leave; absences optionally), statutory settings and year-to-date figures from locked runs; the app computes with `packages/payroll` and stores results with a SHA-256 fingerprint of the inputs; totals are recomputed in the database from the lines; approval needs `payroll.approve`, a different person than the preparer (function check and row constraint), no blocking exceptions, and the fingerprint the checker reproduced; locking makes the run immutable, releases payslips (employees notified) and enables the audited bank file. Locked runs can be purged only in demo organizations (`purge_demo_payroll_run`, for the live tests).
- Performance: goals (the employee, their line and HR maintain them; alignment to a parent goal), review cycles that open one review per active employee with tasks and notifications, self review → manager review (the direct manager, or HR) → calibration (a different final rating needs a note) → shared. Until sharing the employee sees only their self review (`review_view` hides the rest; the raw row isn't readable by the subject); ratings and comments are redacted in the audit trail; 360 feedback requested by the subject or manager, answered once, read anonymously by the subject after sharing.
- Engagement and services (migration 20261018002200): announcements to everyone, a department or a location (scheduled and expiring; publishers see all), acknowledged once by the audience; helpdesk tickets with categories (a category can route to one employee, who works only their tickets), priorities with SLAs (4 h to 3 days), internal notes requesters never see, requester replies that reopen waiting tickets, and a quarter-hourly worker job that flags each missed SLA once and notifies the assignee and helpdesk staff; a knowledge base (published articles for members, drafts for staff, full-text search); kudos with notifications; `colleague_directory`, the minimal directory (name, department, designation of current employees) every member may read, which recruiters now use to choose hiring managers.
- Assets and system access (migration 20261018002300): an asset inventory (auto tags such as AST-0001, unique serial numbers, location, purchase, warranty), issued to a current or joining employee with condition and an optional due-back date; the employee confirms receipt; returning sends it to stock, repair, retirement or lost. A trigger keeps the status `assigned` exactly while an issue is open, and assets with history can only be retired. The access register records accounts per system (who granted and revoked them, and why); a system can name an owner (e.g. the IT lead) who keeps that system's register without HR rights. Employees see their own assets and access; HR sees an "Assets and access" card on each profile (`employee_holdings`, the clearance list Phase 6 offboarding will use). The worker reminds the holder and HR once when a loan is overdue (`worker_asset_returns`, hourly).
- Payroll adjustments: arrears, bonuses and recoveries recorded by payroll staff go into the next run computed (whatever its month), paid in full regardless of loss of pay, kept out of ESI/PT wages, taxable arrears counted once in the TDS projection (engine 1.1.0); locking marks them applied, after which they are history.
- Expenses: categories with receipt rules and per-item limits (Travel, Meals, Local conveyance, Phone and internet, Other by default), claims with items and receipts in the private `expense-receipts` bucket (SHA-256 recorded), duplicate flags at submission (same receipt anywhere, or same date, category and amount), two-step approval (manager, then finance with `expense.manage`), withdrawal back to draft; approved claims flow into the next payroll run as non-taxable reimbursements and are marked paid when that run is locked.
- Daily Core HR upkeep (`worker_daily_core_hr`, hourly): applies future-dated assignment changes on their date, activates joiners on their joining date and emits `employee.joined`; failures are isolated per employee.
- RPCs: `create_employee` (auto codes), `change_employee_assignment`, `create_organization`, `my_permissions`.

**Web** (`apps/web`)
- Auth: password sign-in and email-link sign-in (no self-signup), auth callback, sign out, and a session-refreshing `proxy.ts`.
- Org picker and workspace shell: permission-filtered navigation, mobile navigation, demo banner, skip link.
- Dashboard: headcount by status, upcoming joiners, probation reviews due, "my profile". Figures are scoped to what the viewer may see.
- Employees: directory with search, filters, pagination, CSV export and bulk import; create form with validation; profile page with history timeline, assignment changes, editable work and personal details, emergency contacts, masked identity and bank panels with audited reveal, documents (upload, download, delete), and an activity feed from the audit log.
- Organization structure: list, create, edit, archive and restore departments, designations and locations, with a count of current employees using each.
- Recruitment: offers (make, revise as a new version, submit, send, record the answer), interview scheduling with interviewers, scorecards on My interviews, publish to the careers page; public careers pages at `/careers/<org>`.
- Recruitment: requisitions list with pipeline counts, new requisition form, requisition page with the pipeline by stage (move, hire from Offer, erase), add-candidate form with consent.
- Leave: balances, request form, own requests with cancel, who's away in the next 30 days (team or org), upcoming holidays; Leave settings for HR: types and current rules, new policy versions, holidays, balance adjustments.
- Attendance: check in/out (with location when allowed), this month, regularization requests, team today, flags to review; monthly summary with CSV export; settings for shifts, assignments, geofences, devices and device CSV import.
- Onboarding page (plans with progress, start, template tasks), an onboarding card on the profile, Policies page (read, acknowledge; HR publishes new versions and sees acknowledgements).
- Performance: your goals with progress, your review (self review form, then the shared outcome and anonymous feedback), your team's reviews with the manager form, feedback you've been asked for; Review cycles for HR (create, activate, calibrate per review, rating distribution, share).
- Engagement: announcements with acknowledge buttons and counts for HR; give kudos and the kudos wall. Helpdesk: raise a ticket, your tickets, the open/all queue for staff with SLA flags, a ticket page with the conversation, reply (internal notes for agents), status/priority/assignee, close; category routing for staff. Knowledge base: search, articles, drafts and editing for staff.
- Payroll: runs list and new run, run page (compute, verify and approve, lock, cancel, bank file, results with exceptions, unvalidated-rules warning); Payslips for employees; compensation and statutory settings on the profile.
- Expenses: your claims and a new-claim form, all submitted claims for finance, a claim page (items, receipts, duplicate warnings, add/remove while draft, submit).
- Inbox: approvals the user can decide (approve, or reject with a reason), their open tasks, notifications with an unread badge in the navigation.
- Assignment changes can be future-dated (shown as "Scheduled" in the history until they apply).
- Audit log viewer.
- Platform console: list tenants; create a tenant and invite or attach its owner.

**Shared code**
- `@peoplecore/shared`: zod schemas, permission catalog, date and pagination helpers.
- `@peoplecore/db`: PGlite test harness, generated `Database` types plus a drift test.

**Rollback approach:** migrations are forward-only. A bad schema change is reverted by a new corrective migration. Data recovery relies on Supabase point-in-time recovery, and the restore drill is a Phase 8 deliverable. Destructive migrations (drops, type narrowing) must ship in two steps: expand, then contract.

## Known issues and limitations

1. **End-to-end gaps.** The main journeys are covered by `verify:demo` and `test:e2e` on the live project, and the CI `e2e` job (not yet run) covers the same journeys plus email against a local Supabase. Still missing: real-mailbox delivery on the live project (needs SMTP) and mobile-viewport checks.
2. **RLS performance:** the employee policy calls `app.can_access_employee` per row, and team scope walks a recursive reporting chain. This is fine at small scale. Before the 50k-employees-per-org target, it needs a reporting-closure table or a set-based policy, plus load tests.
3. **The worker isn't deployed anywhere yet.** Scheduled assignment changes and joiner activation only happen while `apps/worker` runs (`corepack pnpm --filter @peoplecore/worker start`, Node 24, with `SUPABASE_URL` and `SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY`). Until it's hosted, run it manually or on a schedule with `WORKER_ONCE=1`.
4. **Page latency:** pages take about 0.7–1.4 s in development. Each request makes roughly five *sequential* Supabase round trips (org lookup, permissions, platform-admin check, page queries) to a project hosted in Seoul. Fix: one RPC returning org + grants + platform flag, parallel page queries, and a Mumbai-region project for Indian customers.
5. The manager picker is capped at 500 employees. Larger tenants need a server-side search combobox.
6. Legal entities and cost centres have no UI yet (the database rules already cover them). Employment history may still reference archived items, by design.
7. **No Content-Security-Policy yet.** A nonce-based CSP needs dedicated work with Next.js (Phase 8). Other security headers are set.
8. `packages/db/src/database.types.ts` is generated by our own PGlite introspection. Compare it with `supabase gen types --linked` now that the project exists.
9. **Dev-only advisory:** `braces` (GHSA-vfj7-8cjw-p6xm, high) reaches the tree via `eslint-config-next`. It's lint tooling only, isn't shipped, and has no patched version yet. CI reports it without blocking; runtime dependencies are blocking.
10. **Supabase Auth settings:** public sign-ups are confirmed **off** on the live project. Still to confirm when email sign-in is tested:
    - Site URL and redirect allowlist include `/auth/callback` (set by the owner, not yet exercised).
    - Invite email template points at `/auth/callback?token_hash={{ .TokenHash }}&type=invite`.
11. **Demo password:** the demo accounts use a 10-character password, accepted with `--allow-short-password` at the owner's request. To strengthen it, change `DEMO_USER_PASSWORD` to 12+ characters and re-run `seed:demo --yes --reset --platform-admin`; existing accounts get the new password.
12. **Process note:** on 2026-10-17 a second agent session (`peoplecore-a6`) wrote into this repo concurrently. Its overlapping files were reconciled into this structure and its duplicates removed. Only one session should write here.
13. **Invitation emails to new people need custom SMTP.** Supabase's built-in email service is heavily rate-limited and meant for testing (it may only deliver to your project team's addresses). Configure custom SMTP before inviting real users. Adding people who already have a PeopleCore sign-in sends no email and works today.

14. **Two-step codes can be reused within their 30-second window** in a different session; Supabase doesn't track used codes (verified on the live project). That's standard for many TOTP setups. Rate limits and short windows limit the risk.
15. **Demo two-step secrets** live in `apps/web/.demo-mfa.json` (gitignored, owner-readable only). They belong to synthetic demo accounts; real users enroll their own authenticator app.
16. **Orphaned document files are possible but unreachable.** If removing a stored file fails (after a delete, or after the database refused a new document), the file stays in the bucket. No signed URL is ever issued for it, since the database has no live record. A cleanup job (worker, Phase 3) should sweep the bucket against `employee_documents`.
17. **Documents aren't scanned for malware.** Types are checked by their bytes and limited to PDF, JPEG and PNG, and downloads are served as attachments, but there is no antivirus scan yet.
18. ~~**Recruiters can't choose a hiring manager.**~~ Addressed: without directory access the picker (and the names shown) come from `colleague_directory` (name, department, designation only). The directory caps at 500 people, so large tenants still need a search combobox.
19. **No demo recruiter account.** The live journeys use HR (who can both run recruitment and hire). Adding `demo.recruiter` means re-running `seed:demo`; the database tests cover the recruiter role.
20. **Recruitment gaps:** candidates answer offers outside PeopleCore (a recruiter records the answer); no resume upload or parsing yet (Phase 7 AI adapter); no background verification or consent expiry; the careers page has no edge rate limiting (Phase 8). RPC parameters without SQL defaults must be sent as explicit nulls (`sqlNull`), which the browser tests caught.
21. **Leave accrual only credits the current period.** If the worker doesn't run during a whole month, that month's accrual is skipped (HR can adjust). The year-end lapse check runs each hour for every employee and type: fine at demo scale, to be narrowed before large tenants.
22. **Not yet in leave:** compensatory off, encashment, sandwich rules beyond "count every day", per-location calendars in the UI (the database supports them), and the attendance link (approved leave marking attendance days) which comes with attendance.
23. **Attendance recomputation is row-by-row.** Each punch recomputes its day (and the day before), and the monthly summary recomputes every day of the month for every employee before summarizing. Fine at demo scale; large device imports and big tenants need set-based recomputation and a locked summary per payroll period (Phase 4).
24. **Payroll is NOT ready for real salaries.** The statutory rule sets are encoded from public information and marked unvalidated; a qualified professional must review the rules and the golden vectors and sign off (set `validated`) before production use (roadmap Phase 4 requirement). Also missing: mid-month compensation changes (the record on the last day of the month applies), old tax regime and investment declarations, surcharge (refused with a blocking exception above ₹50 lakh), PT for states other than Karnataka and Maharashtra (blocking exception), payslip PDF, and expenses.
25. ~~**Confirmations disappear with the form they belong to.**~~ Addressed: actions whose form unmounts (approve/lock/cancel a run, move an application, share a cycle, complete a task, cancel leave, archive structure, delete a document…) set a short-lived `pc_flash` cookie (`server/flash.ts`, 30 s, httpOnly) that the org layout shows once as a success alert. Forms that stay on the page keep their inline message only, so nothing shows twice.
26. **Helpdesk and engagement gaps:** no email for ticket replies yet (in-app notifications only), no attachments on tickets, SLAs run on the clock (no business hours or pause while waiting), no CSAT survey; announcements have no rich text or images; the knowledge base is plain text and not yet used by the AI assistant (Phase 7, with the reader's permissions).
27. **Asset and access gaps:** no bulk asset import or CSV export yet, no depreciation or QR labels, and employees request equipment through the helpdesk rather than a dedicated request flow. The access register is a record of accounts, not a provisioning integration (SCIM/SSO connectors are Phase 8). The live demo tenant gets demo assets, systems and the seeded announcement and articles only after `seed:demo --reset`.
